# Breakout Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add Breakout as the third Retro game: 30 fixed, varied maps dealt in shuffled order, special bricks and power-ups, played until the lives run out, with a glowing canvas field.

**Architecture:** three separate layers in `games/games.js`:
- **maps:** `breakoutMakers` / `breakoutBuild` / `breakoutCheck` / `breakoutDeck` / `BREAKOUT_MAPS`, pure;
- **physics:** `breakoutNew` / `breakoutStep`, pure, sub-stepped;
- **drawing:** `breakoutDraw` on a canvas, driven by `BreakoutGame`'s rAF loop, which also turns step `events` into sounds and effects.

**Tech Stack:** plain JS in `games/games.js` (React via `window.__oneKit`), Canvas 2D; tests use `node:test` + Playwright through `tests/helpers.cjs` (`waitForGames`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-29-breakout-design.md`

**Depends on:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-29-fast-loading.md` (games already live in `games/`).

## Global Constraints

- Field 480×640. Bricks are 36×16 with 4 px gaps, 12 columns × up to 12 rows, starting at x = 12, y = 56.
- Paddle: 88 px (132 during Wide), 12 px tall, at y = 600, moving 520 px/s with keys. Ball: radius 7 at 340 px/s (238 during Slow).
- Paddle bounce: the angle from vertical is `offset × 60°`. The ball is never flatter than 15° from horizontal.
- `dt` is capped at 50 ms, with sub-steps of at most 4 px of ball travel.
- Points:
  - +10 per hit;
  - +10 × starting hp when a brick breaks;
  - +20 per explosive;
  - +500 per cleared map.
- Lives start at 3, at most 5.
- Power-ups:
  - capsules fall at 140 px/s;
  - Wide lasts 15000 ms and Slow 12000 ms;
  - Multi adds 2 balls at ±25°;
  - the kind weights are Wide 3 / Multi 3 / Slow 3 / Life 1.
- The map-cleared pause is 1500 ms. Tiers are 10 gentle / 12 medium / 8 tough, with the mix ranges from the spec table.
- No level number or map number appears anywhere. The best score is saved with `useGameRecord("breakout")`.
- Copy fixed by the spec:
  - names: "Breakout", "Break every brick, catch the power-ups.", "🏓", "Score", "Lives", "Best";
  - messages: "Ready?", "Play", "Paused", "Resume", "Restart", "Map cleared!", "Game over", "Play again", "🏆 New record!", "Game sounds".

## Review Focus

1. **Two balls hitting the same 1-hp brick in one step:** the brick breaks once and scores once, with no negative hp and no double capsule. *(Task 2 test)*
2. **A chain of explosives:** each brick is processed once and an explosive is never re-triggered, so a loop of explosives can't recurse forever. *(Task 2 test)*
3. **A ball wedged between steel bricks:** it never gets stuck oscillating in place forever. A ball whose position barely changes for 3 s of steps gets a nudge, or the min-angle rule frees it. *(Task 2 test)*
4. **The mouse leaving the field, or a switch from touch to keys:** the paddle doesn't jump to a stale target, and keys work right after the mouse leaves. *(Task 3 test)*
5. **Losing the last ball on the same step the last brick breaks:** clearing wins. The map counts as cleared, and no life is lost. *(Task 2 test)*

- Produces:

  - `breakoutMakers: { wall, twin, mirror, pyramid, diamonds, stripes, pillars, fortress, caves, waves }`. Each is `(rng: () => number, params) -> Set<"c,r">`, using the params named in the spec.

  - `BREAKOUT_MAPS: Def[30]`, each `{ id, maker, params, seed, tier, mix: { strong, maxHp, steel, explosive, bonus }, palette }`.
  - `BREAKOUT_FALLBACK`: a gentle wall def.

### Task 2: Physics and the game loop

**Files:**
- Modify: `games/games.js` (`/* Breakout physics */`)
- Test: `tests/breakout.test.cjs`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Task 1.
- Produces:
  - `breakoutNew(seed) -> State`: `{ rng, deck, deckIndex, mapId, bricks, paddle: { x, w }, balls: [{ x, y, vx, vy, stuck }], capsules: [], effects: { wide: 0, slow: 0 }, score: 0, lives: 3, status: "ready", clearMs: 0, events: [] }`.
  - `breakoutStep(state, dtMs, input: { targetX: number|null, dir: -1|0|1, launch: boolean }) -> State`. Each call starts with `events: []`, and events are `{ type, ... }` with `type ∈ paddle|hit|break|steel|boom|catch|lost|life|cleared|over`. `hit` and `break` carry `row`.
    - In `ready`, launch → `playing` with the stuck ball sent up.
    - `paused` and `over` return the state unchanged.
    - In `cleared`, `clearMs` counts up; at ≥ 1500 the next map is dealt and the status goes back to `ready`.
  - `breakoutWithBricks(state, bricks)`: a test helper that swaps the bricks.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests.** Each builds a state with `breakoutNew(1)`, optionally calls `breakoutWithBricks`, places the balls by hand and steps.
  - `walls and top bounce`:
    - a ball moving left at x = 10 ends with `vx > 0`;
    - one moving up at y = 10 ends with `vy > 0`;
    - the speed is the same (±0.5).
  - `paddle centre goes straight up, edge goes out at ~60°`: aim a ball at the paddle centre, then at offset 0.95. The angles from vertical are about 0° and about 57° (±3°), and the speed is kept.
  - `a fast ball never passes through a brick`: with a ball at 3× speed straight up at one brick, after one 50 ms step the brick took a hit and the ball points down.
  - `strong bricks lose hp, steel never breaks`:
    - a 3-hp brick hit 3 times breaks on the 3rd, and the score is 10 + 10 + 10 + 30 = 60;
    - a steel brick hit 5 times is still there, the score is 0, and 5 `steel` events fired.
  - `explosions break neighbours and spare steel, chains run once` (Review Focus 2): a 3×3 block with an explosive in the centre, one explosive neighbour and one steel corner. Hitting the centre breaks every non-steel brick once. The `boom` events = 2, steel remains, and the score includes +20 × 2.
  - `two balls on one brick break it once` (Review Focus 1): two balls that both overlap a 1-hp bonus brick in the same step give one `break` event and at most one capsule.
  - `power-ups work and expire`:
    - Wide → `paddle.w = 132`, back to 88 after 15000 ms of steps;
    - Slow → the speed is 238, and 340 after 12000 ms;
    - Multi → 3 balls;
    - Life at 5 lives stays 5.
  - `losing the last ball costs a life; none left is game over`:
    - one ball below 640 gives lives − 1, a `lost` event, a new stuck ball, and cleared capsules and effects;
    - with `lives: 1` the result is `status: "over"` and an `over` event.
  - `clearing wins over losing the last ball` (Review Focus 5): in one step the only ball breaks the last brick and another, already-lost ball falls out. The status is `cleared` and the lives are unchanged.
  - `a cleared map keeps score and lives and deals the next one`: clear a map → +500 and `cleared`. After 1500 ms of steps the status is `ready`, `mapId` is the next deck id, the bricks are rebuilt, and the score and lives are the same.
  - `the ball is never flat`: launch a ball nearly horizontal (5°), step once, and its angle from horizontal is ≥ 15°.
  - `a ball wedged between steel frees itself` (Review Focus 3): a ball bouncing between two steel bricks 1 px wider than it is, after 3000 ms of 16 ms steps, has a different row band (|Δy| > 20).
- [ ] **Step 2: Run** `cd tests && node --test breakout.test.cjs`. Expected: the new tests fail.
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement the Interfaces above.**
  - Collisions are circle-against-box with the closest point. The ball reflects on the axis with the smaller overlap and is pushed out along it.
  - Hits are collected per sub-step into a Set of bricks, so a brick takes at most 1 damage per sub-step.
  - Explosions use a queue and a visited set.
  - Breakable bricks are counted after the brick phase and before balls are removed, so clearing wins (Review Focus 5).
  - The stall rule: when a ball has touched only steel or walls for 3000 ms, rotate its velocity by 20° towards the paddle.
- [ ] **Step 4: Run** `cd tests && node --test breakout.test.cjs`. Expected: all pass.
- [ ] **Step 5: Commit** `git commit -am "feat: Breakout physics — bounces, special bricks, power-ups, lives, map clears"`
